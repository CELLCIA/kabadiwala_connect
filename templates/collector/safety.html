Safety Center

Best practices for handling e-waste safely.

Don't Burn Cables

Burning cables releases toxic fumes that harm your lungs and the environment. Sell them as is.

Avoid Acid Usage

Using acid to extract metals is dangerous without proper facility and gear.

Handle Batteries Carefully

Lithium batteries can catch fire if punctured. Store them in a cool, dry place.

CRT Monitors

Old monitors contain lead glass. Do not break the glass tubes.

Protective Gear

Always wear gloves and masks when handling dusty or sharp components.
